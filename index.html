Questions people ask

What does a fractional CTO do?

A fractional CTO gives you senior technical leadership for a few days a month instead of a full-time hire. I make or unblock the technology decisions, set direction for the team, and help you hire a permanent CTO when you're ready.

When does a company need a fractional CTO rather than a full-time one?

When the technical decisions are bigger than the budget for a full-time CTO. Common moments are before a funding round, a first engineering hire, a platform rebuild, or an AI feature going to customers.

What is AI governance and assurance?

It's how you show an AI feature is safe to ship, and that it stays safe afterwards. I set the evidence it has to produce before release, who signs it off, and how you spot drift once it's live.

What should a board ask for before approving an AI project?

Evidence, not a demo. A board should see what the system has to prove, how it was tested, who owns it when it goes wrong, and what it costs to run each year.

Do you work with charities and small businesses?

Yes. I sit on charity boards and work with makers and small teams, and the advice is sized to the budget, which often means fewer tools, not more.
