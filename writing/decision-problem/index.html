The decision problem

Martin Jackson · 18 September 2026

Most engineering organisations I see don't have a delivery problem.

They have a decision problem.

The symptoms usually appear technical:

  • too much technical debt
  • missed roadmap commitments
  • slow releases
  • frustrated engineers

But underneath, I often find something else:

unclear priorities, too much work in progress, slow decisions, and teams optimising locally rather than for the business outcome.

I have watched this up close. A decision everyone thought was in flight had never actually been asked. The ask was buried on slide 34. Nobody said no. Nobody said yes. Everyone assumed the silence meant something.

Fixing the CI/CD pipeline might improve deployment speed.

It won't fix an organisation that cannot decide what matters.

The job of technology leadership is not simply to improve engineering.

It is to identify the constraint preventing engineering from producing business value.
